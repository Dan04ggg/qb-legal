Privacy Policy

Field Sales Bot · Last updated 11 November 2026

Field Sales Bot (“the App”) is a private tool operated by DTM Ventures Ltd for its own internal use. This policy explains what data the App handles.

What we access from QuickBooks

With the account holder’s permission, the App reads the QuickBooks chart of accounts and creates deposit transactions recording our own income. It does not read customers, invoices, payroll or any other QuickBooks data.

What we store

The App stores the QuickBooks access tokens needed to stay connected, and the business records we enter ourselves (appointments, sales and payments). This data is held on a private, access-controlled server in our control, and in a private Google Sheet.

Sharing

We do not sell or share data with third parties. Text and images we submit for reading may be processed by AI service providers (Google, DeepSeek) solely to extract the information we need.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only as long as needed for our business and accounting records. Disconnecting the App in QuickBooks revokes its access immediately. Stored tokens are deleted on request.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data we hold about you.
